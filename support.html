What Phase is

Phase (QUANTA 05) is a 3D puzzle game about quantum tunneling. You charge up your odds and tunnel through panes of glass, gather energy from wells, and go down through thin floor to walk upside down along the underside of the world. It has eight chapters, a finale and an Endless mode. The game plays offline. Ads and the store need a connection.

How to play

  • Move with the thumbstick on the left half of the screen. Drag on the right half to look around.
  • Face a pane of glass, hold the Tunnel button to charge, then let go. A ring in the middle shows your odds.
  • Each membrane in your way costs one energy. Step into an energy well to gain energy; each well works once.
  • On thin floor, tunnel down to the underside, where stone walls don't reach. It costs one energy each way.
  • The small round button puts the room back the way it started.

Common problems

Blank or black screen. Close the app fully and open it again. If it stays blank, restart your device and install any update for Phase and for your system. If it happens again, email us.

No sound. Turn up the media volume, and check silent mode and any connected headphones. Sound levels are in Settings in the game.

Lost progress. Progress is saved on your device. We have no accounts and can't see or recover it. Deleting the app deletes its saved progress.

A video gave no reward. Continue in Endless needs the video to finish. If no video is available, check your connection and try again later.

Restoring purchases

  • Android: use the Google account that made the purchase, open Settings in Phase and tap Restore purchase.
  • iPhone and iPad: use the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Settings in Phase and tap Restore purchases.

Purchases belong to the store where you bought them, so they don't move between Android and Apple devices.

Refunds

The store takes the payment and decides on refunds.

  • Android: request a Google Play refund.
  • iPhone and iPad: request an Apple refund.

Ads and privacy choices

Phase is free with ads. The video for Continue in Endless is optional, and an ad can appear between chapters. Remove ads stops the ads between chapters and makes Continue free.

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: allow or stop tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where Google's consent rules require it, an Ad privacy choices button appears in the game's Settings.
